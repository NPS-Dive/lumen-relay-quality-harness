## How to publish

Post from your personal profile, not a company page. Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday morning in the region you are targeting works best. For EU roles, that is Central European morning.

The first two lines have to carry the point. LinkedIn hides the rest behind “see more”.

After the post is live:

1. Paste the GitHub URL as the first comment. A link in the post body is shown to fewer people.
2. Add the repository to the Featured section of your profile.
3. Reply to every comment in the first hour.

Use only these three hashtags: `#QualityAssurance` `#TestAutomation` `#k6`.

Do not ask people to comment a word or to tag friends. LinkedIn discounts that pattern.
